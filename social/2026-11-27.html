How this is measured

Public posts mentioning the cruise lines are gathered from X, Threads, Bluesky, Instagram and Facebook inside the window, then each post is rated positive, neutral or negative (12 rated by the daily brief's reviewer, 0 by the fallback word list). Net sentiment is positives minus negatives as a share of all posts, from −100 to +100; the weighted figure applies the per-platform weights. Ships and destinations are recognised from the lists in the backend config, and a ship or place is called out as a standout when it draws enough mentions with a clearly positive or negative tilt. Brands, ships, destinations, watch topics, platform weights and standout thresholds are all adjustable in config/social_listening.json. Sample sizes are small, so treat single-day scores as a pulse, not a poll.
