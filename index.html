Cruise brief — Tue Nov 10, 2026

Covering Mon Nov 9 – Tue Nov 10, 2026

Top 3

  1. CCL RCL NCLH Cruise stocks climbed again Monday as crude fell about 3.7% to near $87. Carnival rose about 3.5% to around $30.60, Royal Caribbean about 3.4% to about $338, and Norwegian about 2.5% to about $18.36. Cruise Stocks Climb Amid Falling Crude Prices
  2. Princess put its 2028–29 Australia and New Zealand season and the 129-night 2029 Grand World Odyssey on Grand Princess on sale today. The early booking bonus, which includes up to $3,000 in onboard credit, runs through Feb 16, 2027. Princess Cruises Unveils 2029 World Cruise Alongside 2028/29 Australia, New Zealand Season
  3. Authorities ordered MSC Seascape back to Galveston a day into a seven-night sailing after a suspected homicide on board. A 48-year-old Texas man was arrested, and the ship resumed its itinerary Monday afternoon. 48-Year-Old Texas Man Arrested After Suspected Homicide Aboard MSC Seascape

Carnival Corp & brands

Carnival Cruise Line

  • Carnival Luminosa's sold-out 17-night adults-only sailing from Singapore on Nov 13 drops several Platinum perks, including priority embarkation and early cabin access, because so many Platinum guests are booked. Three Southeast Asia calls were also reshuffled. Carnival Luminosa Cuts Platinum Perks on Adults-Only Cruise, Shuffles Ports
  • Guests say the ink on Carnival's new Sail & Sign cards rubs off within a day or two, which has meant repeat reprints at Guest Services. Carnival Guests Say Ink Is Rubbing Off New Sail & Sign Cards Within Days
  • For Veterans Day, Carnival pledged $300,000 to military-family causes across seven homeports and hosted an Operation Homefront event on Carnival Sunshine in Norfolk. Carnival Honors Veterans and Military Families Across U.S. Homeports

Princess

The 2028–29 Australia and New Zealand program has more than 90 departures on Grand, Emerald and Royal Princess, and Emerald Princess returns Down Under for the first time since 2016–17 (see Top 3).

Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings

  • NCLH Shares gained about 2.5% Monday along with the rest of the sector, but the stock is still down about 18% this year (see Top 3).
  • A U.S. Coast Guard inspection will push Norwegian Dawn's Nov 15 departure from Jacksonville back two hours, to 5 p.m., on its first U.S. sailing after a summer in Europe. Coast Guard Inspection to Delay Norwegian Dawn Departure From Jacksonville

Royal Caribbean Group

  • RCL RCL led Monday's oil-driven rally, though it hedges about 58% of its Q4 fuel, so cheaper crude helps its earnings less than it helps Carnival's.
  • Legend of the Seas is the first Icon-class ship based at Port Everglades. It sails six-night Western Caribbean and eight-night Southern Caribbean itineraries, all with a Perfect Day CocoCay call. Royal Caribbean's Legend of the Seas Makes U.S. Debut
  • Dream Vacations and CruiseOne expect about $1 billion in combined 2026 sales, a figure they announced at their conference aboard Star of the Seas. Dream Vacations and CruiseOne Ending 2026 with $1B Sales Milestone

Industry-wide

  • Weather concerns moved the SS United States sinking up a day to Nov 11 (Veterans Day) at 1 p.m. CST off Destin, and the sendoff ceremony is canceled. SS United States Sinking Moved Up Over Weather Concerns
  • A 24-hour general strike in Spain on Nov 11 led MSC Seaview to replace its Ibiza call with a sea day. MSC Seaview Cancels Ibiza Call Due to Spanish General Strike
  • HX Expeditions CEO Gebhard Rainer will step down after a transition of a few months. HX says year-to-date bookings are up more than 25%. HX Expeditions CEO Gebhard Rainer to Step Down
  • Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection named Leibman as CEO and Ferreira as president. Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection Names Leibman CEO, Ferreira President

Social buzz

Carnival's flaking Sail & Sign cards and the Platinum perk cuts on Luminosa are drawing complaints from loyal guests on cruise forums. Passenger accounts of the MSC Seascape lockdown spread widely before officials confirmed any details.
