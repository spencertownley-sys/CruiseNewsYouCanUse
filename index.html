Cruise brief — Mon Nov 23, 2026

Covering Fri Nov 20 – Sun Nov 22, 2026

Top 3

  1. Princess Plus and Premier package perks, which cover drinks, dining and Wi-Fi, now extend to Carnival Corp's private destinations. They apply at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay from today, Nov 23, and across Celebration Key from Feb 1, 2027. Carnival is making a similar change for its own drink packages. Princess Extends Package Perks to Celebration Key and Half Moon Cay
  2. CCL RCL NCLH Cruise stocks closed lower on Friday, extending a pullback that started Thursday. NCLH ended near $16, down about 5–6%, with RCL near $296 and CCL near $26.60. Reports disagree on the exact moves and we found no single cause. Morningstar RCL
  3. Seabourn's Black Friday sale runs Nov 17 through Dec 8 and covers nearly 780 sailings into mid-2029. It offers two free Veranda Suite category upgrades, up to $2,000 in shipboard credit per suite and 15% deposits. Seabourn's Black Friday Sale
