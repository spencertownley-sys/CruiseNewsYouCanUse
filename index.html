Cruise brief — Fri Nov 20, 2026

Covering Thu Nov 19 – Fri Nov 20, 2026

Top 3

  1. CCL Carnival released new details on its next-generation Ace Class, first named in July with Carnival Destiny. The ships are smaller than recent Carnival builds: LNG-powered, about 130,000 gross tons with roughly 1,650 cabins, and sized to reach more ports. Fincantieri will build two at Marghera for delivery in 2033 and 2035 under a contract worth more than €2 billion, and the design is meant to work for several Carnival Corp brands. Next Gen Carnival Ships Downsize to 3,300 Guests
  2. Carnival is also expanding Celebration Key by 6,000 guests of land-side capacity, in two phases in 2028 and 2030. The 2030 phase adds a water park with 18 waterslides, a lazy river and an infinity pool. Carnival Destiny, due in 2029, will have eight pools, more than any other ship in the fleet. Carnival Cruise Line Announces New Ship Class, Celebration Key Expansion
  3. Holland America will send Eurodam to Asia for the first time in 2028–29. The season runs September 2028 to May 2029, with 11- to 27-day sailings from Tokyo, Hong Kong and Singapore, five new ports, and up to $400 in onboard credit for Mariner Society members who book early. Eurodam to Make Asia Debut for Holland America in 2028-29
