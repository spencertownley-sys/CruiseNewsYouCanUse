Cruise brief — Fri Oct 30, 2026

Covering Thu Oct 29, 2026

Top 3

  1. NCLH Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings beat on Q3: adjusted EPS was $1.12, against $0.90 guided and a ~$0.94 consensus, and revenue of about $2.95 billion topped the ~$2.88 billion estimate. Full-year adjusted EPS guidance rose to about $1.70 from $1.50, and the shares jumped roughly 11–13%. Norwegian Cruise Line Soars 11% on Raised Guidance as CEO Sees "Meaningful Acceleration" in 2027
  2. NCLH CFO Mark Kempa, who has been with the company for more than 25 years, is stepping down. Chief Strategy Officer Meghan Montana, formerly of American Airlines, becomes CFO on Jan 1, 2027, and Kempa stays on as an adviser through June 2027. Mark Kempa Out as NCLH CFO; Meghan Montana to Take Role
  3. Carnival Luminosa will homeport in Baltimore from November 2028, after leaving Australia, and will sail alongside Carnival Miracle on 6- to 14-day Bahamas, Bermuda and Caribbean trips. Carnival Pride does not appear in the 2028–29 Baltimore schedule. Carnival Luminosa Will Homeport in Baltimore After Australia Exit

Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings

  • The quarter: Net yield fell 6.5% as reported (6.7% in constant currency), about 220 bps better than guidance, and adjusted EBITDA of $937 million beat the guide by $63 million. CEO John Chidsey credited unusually strong close-in demand. Late Bookers Lift Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Past Q3 Guidance
  • Outlook: Q4 constant-currency net yield guidance is unchanged at about -6.5%, and it assumes late bookings will not be as strong again. The delay of Seven Seas Prestige also weighs on Q4. NCLH expects 2027 net yield growth of 1–2%, weighted to the second half, and its booked position for the first half of 2027 is behind last year, though at higher prices. Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
  • Costs and balance sheet: NCLH found another $100 million in run-rate savings, mainly from guest and crew air, bringing the total identified so far to more than $600 million. Net leverage stands at 5.5x. NCLH Identifies Another $100 Million in Savings, More Coming
  • Regent: Delivery of Seven Seas Prestige has slipped to late December, and its first bookable sailing is now Dec 28 from Miami. Regent Quietly Delays Seven Seas Prestige, Cancels Maiden Voyage

Royal Caribbean Group

  • Liberty of the Seas is repositioning to Galveston after a Royal Amplified refit and will run year-round 4- and 5-night Western Caribbean cruises through April 2028. Royal Caribbean's Liberty Returning to North America After Refit
  • TUI Cruises picked Lloyd Werft Bremerhaven to repair Mein Schiff 3 and expects to decide early next week whether later sailings are affected. Lloyd Werft Bremerhaven Chosen for Mein Schiff 3 Repairs

Industry-wide

  • Villa Vie Residences plans to file for Chapter 11 after leaving more than 200 residents in Cambodia. It blamed fuel costs that rose after the Iran war began in February. Villa Vie Set to File Chapter 11 After Odyssey Residents Left in Cambodia
  • MSC texted guests one day before embarkation to say an MSC Opera sailing was cancelled for technical reasons, and promised full refunds plus reimbursement of expenses. MSC Opera Guests Get Cancellation Texts Just One Day Before Sailing
  • Viking took delivery of the 190-guest river ship Viking Haki and expects to have 114 river ships by 2028. Viking Welcomes Newest River Ship, 190-Guest Haki

Social buzz

Carnival's "Crew love" sticker split cruisers: some like it, but many commenters called it tacky and worry that guests who prepay will get better service.
