Cruise brief — Mon Nov 9, 2026

Covering Fri Nov 6 – Sun Nov 8, 2026

Top 3

  1. Princess's Blue Friday sale runs through Dec 7, with up to 50% off fares, up to $400 in instant savings and free fares for third and fourth guests on select sailings. A past-guest Black Friday Private Sale on Nov 13–23 adds up to $800 off and 50% off deposits. Blue Is Back: Princess Cruises Unveils Blue Friday Savings
  2. Holland America is spending $70 million to rebuild its Denali Lodge, which reopens in May 2027 with 192 new rooms in two new buildings. Six HAL ships will run more than 100 Alaska cruises in 2027. Holland America's Reimagined Denali Lodge Gets 192 New Rooms
  3. Silversea opened The Cormorant at 55 South, a 150-room hotel in Puerto Williams, Chile, that now serves as the gateway for its Antarctica fly-cruises. Its season runs 40 voyages through March 2027, 29 of them fly-cruises. Silversea Opens Cormorant at 55

Carnival Corp & brands

Carnival Cruise Line

Carnival has now moved sailaway earlier on 316 sailings after adding 47 Carnival Pride sailings from Baltimore and 23 Carnival Adventure sailings from Sydney. Carnival Earlier Departures Now Hit 316 Cruises

Princess

Sun Princess is crossing the Atlantic and arrives in Fort Lauderdale on Nov 21. Its winter Caribbean season opens with a six-night trip that calls at Celebration Key. Sun Princess Starts Repositioning Voyage to Florida

Holland America

Holland America will offer 19 Alaska Cruisetours in 2027 that pair a cruise with time in Denali and the Yukon (see Top 3).

Seabourn

Seabourn refreshed the wine list included in its fares, which now has more than 40 wines from 10 countries plus wines made specifically for the line. Seabourn Refreshes Inclusive Wine Collection Across Fleet

Other Carnival brands

  • P&O Cruises' Black Friday sale offers up to £500 off per cabin through Dec 1 and comes with a price-drop guarantee. P&O Cruises Unveils Black Friday Savings and Price Drop Guarantee
  • Costa joined AIDA in signing a 10-year fly-cruise deal for La Romana, where Costa Fascinosa is based this winter. La Romana Secures Decade-Long Fly & Cruise Partnership with Costa

Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings

Regent announced six original production shows for Seven Seas Prestige and confirmed its maiden voyage leaves Miami on Dec 28. A Broadway Experience program starts on the ship on Jan 20, 2027. Six Original Shows, Broadway Stars Coming to Seven Seas Prestige

Royal Caribbean Group

  • Legend of the Seas reached Port Everglades on Nov 7, its first U.S. call. It will be named on Nov 11 and starts regular eight- and six-night sailings on Nov 14. Royal Caribbean's Legend of the Seas Arrives in the U.S. for First Time
  • Fishermen rescued an Ovation of the Seas crew member, a pianist, about 15–20 miles off Southern California. He is expected to survive, and the Coast Guard is investigating how he went overboard. Ovation of the Seas Crew Member Rescued by Fishermen Off California

Industry-wide

  • Singapore authorities arrested Villa Vie's Odyssey on Nov 8 over what appears to be a creditor claim. No residents are aboard, and the ship could go to a judicial sale. Villa Vie Odyssey Arrested in Singapore With No Residents Onboard
  • A federal judge denied a last-minute restraining order to stop the SS United States sinking. The tow is set to start Nov 9, with the sinking planned for Nov 12 off Destin, Florida. Court Rejects Last-Minute Bid to Stop SS United States Sinking
  • Adora Cruises took delivery of Adora Flora City, a 5,232-guest ship and the second Adora has had built in China. It will sail from Guangzhou starting Nov 22. Adora Takes Delivery of Flora City
