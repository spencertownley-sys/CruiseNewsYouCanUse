# Cruise brief — Mon Oct 5, 2026
*Covering roughly Fri Oct 2 – Sun Oct 4, 2026*

## Top 3
1. [RCL] Royal Caribbean shares rallied 17% over the week on growing enthusiasm for the Sandals resorts joint venture; Citi dropped its 90-day upside catalyst watch (Buy, $362 target), saying the move is now priced in.
2. [NCLH] Norwegian jumped roughly 3.5% Friday as its $750M senior notes offering (refinancing 2028 debt) closed and sector demand sentiment stayed firm.
3. Hurricane Rachel kept disrupting Mexican Riviera sailings over the weekend, hitting Carnival and Princess directly — Grand Princess lost its Cabo San Lucas call Oct 2, and Carnival Panorama made a second itinerary change before returning to Long Beach Oct 4.

## Carnival Corp & brands

### Carnival Cruise Line
Carnival Radiance returned to Long Beach on Oct 3 after a 15-day drydock at Seaspan in Victoria — refreshed interiors, an upgraded Effy jewelry boutique, and a new "From Sea to Shining Sea" bow crest marking the ship's 25th year in service. [Carnival Radiance back in Long Beach after drydock (Cruise Industry News)](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/carnival-radiance-back-in-long-beach-after-drydock/)

Carnival Panorama had to drop additional Mexican ports as Hurricane Rachel approached, calling twice at Ensenada instead before returning to Long Beach on Oct 4; affected guests received $300 onboard credit per stateroom. [Carnival Panorama: second itinerary change due to hurricane (Cruise Industry News)](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/carnival-panorama-second-itinerary-change-due-to-hurricane/)

## Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings
[NCLH] Shares rose roughly 3.5% Friday as the $750M senior notes offering (due 2031) closed, funding redemption of the 6.125% 2028 notes and paying down credit lines — investors read it as proactive balance-sheet management rather than a dilution worry, with the company reaffirming full-year EPS guidance of about $1.50. [NCLH rallies as debt move and cruise demand lift outlook (StocksToTrade)](https://stockstotrade.com/news/norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-ltd-nclh-news-2026_10_02/)

## Industry-wide
MSC Cruises floated out MSC World Atlantic at its French shipyard — the line's 25th ship, carrying up to 6,758 guests — ahead of a November 2027 debut sailing Caribbean itineraries from Port Canaveral. [Port Canaveral's next mega cruise ship (ClickOrlando)](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/10/04/port-canaverals-next-mega-cruise-ship-is-a-step-closer-to-sailing-meet-the-msc-world-atlantic)

Virgin Voyages' Scarlet Lady broke free from its moorings in Rhodes, Greece on Sept 30 (reported Oct 2) amid heavy swells, clipping a catamaran and two smaller boats; no injuries were reported and the ship returned to berth under its own power with tug assistance.
