# Cruise brief — Tue Nov 10, 2026
*Covering Mon Nov 9 – Tue Nov 10, 2026*

## Top 3
1. [CCL] [RCL] [NCLH] Cruise stocks climbed again Monday as crude fell about 3.7% to near $87. Carnival rose about 3.5% to around $30.60, Royal Caribbean about 3.4% to about $338, and Norwegian about 2.5% to about $18.36. [Cruise Stocks Climb Amid Falling Crude Prices](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2026/11/09/cruise-stocks-climb-amid-falling-crude-prices)

The early booking bonus, which includes up to $3,000 in onboard credit, runs through Feb 16, 2027. [Princess Cruises Unveils 2029 World Cruise Alongside 2028/29 Australia, New Zealand Season](https://www.travelmarketreport.com/cruises/articles/princess-cruises-2029-world-cruise)
3. Authorities ordered MSC Seascape back to Galveston a day into a seven-night sailing after a suspected homicide on board. A 48-year-old Texas man was arrested, and the ship resumed its itinerary Monday afternoon. [48-Year-Old Texas Man Arrested After Suspected Homicide Aboard MSC Seascape](https://www.cruisehive.com/48-year-old-texas-man-arrested-after-suspected-homicide-aboard-msc-seascape/223266)
