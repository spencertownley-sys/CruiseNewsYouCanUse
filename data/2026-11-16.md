# Cruise brief — Mon Nov 16, 2026
*Covering Fri Nov 13 – Sun Nov 15, 2026*

## Top 3
1. An FAA telecom failure halted all U.S. flights for about four hours on Friday, and roughly 12,000 flights were canceled. On Saturday, ships including Star Princess, Eurodam, Carnival Magic, Legend and Icon of the Seas, Norwegian Luna and MSC World America left two to four hours late so stranded guests could board. [Cruise Lines Postpone Departures After U.S. Flight Halt](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/cruise-lines-postpone-departures-after-u-s-flight-halt/)
2. A tanker struck American Cruise Lines' riverboat American Heritage while it was docked in New Orleans on Friday and passengers were getting off, and the boat capsized. Everyone was accounted for, and 32 people were hospitalized, none critically. Carnival's New Orleans sailings ran as planned. [32 Hospitalized, None Missing After the American Heritage Capsizes](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/32-hospitalized-none-missing-after-the-american-heritage-capsizes/)
3. The U.S. Coast Guard has opened a criminal investigation into MSC Poesia over an alleged "magic pipe" that sent oily waste into the gray-water system. The ship was held in PortMiami from Nov 1 to Nov 12, which canceled an 11-night sailing. [Coast Guard Opens Criminal Probe Into MSC Poesia](https://www.cruisehive.com/coast-guard-opens-criminal-probe-into-msc-poesia-over-alleged-magic-pipe/223581)
