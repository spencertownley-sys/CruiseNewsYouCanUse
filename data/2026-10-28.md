## Royal Caribbean Group
- **The quarter:** Revenue was $5.59 billion, up 9%, which was just short of the ~$5.61 billion consensus. Net yields rose 0.9% as reported (0.6% in constant currency), helped by stronger-than-expected close-in demand for Europe and the Caribbean and by onboard spending. Net cruise costs excluding fuel fell 1.9%, mostly because spending shifted into Q4. Load factor was 112%.
- **Guidance:** Full-year net yields are now expected to rise about 2.7% as reported (2.1% in constant currency). Q3 strength drove the raise, partly offset by higher fuel costs and currency. Q4 guidance calls for net yields up 4.2–4.7% in constant currency, but Q4 adjusted EPS of $3.48–$3.58 is below the ~$3.65–$3.66 consensus. [Royal Caribbean raises 2026 earnings outlook as Q3 revenue climbs](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/passenger/cruise/royal-caribbean-raises-earnings-outlook)
- **Demand signals:** Close to half of 2026 guests were repeat customers, and half of all guests are now millennials or younger. Liberty said cruise pricing still has room to rise compared with land vacations. Management reaffirmed its Perfecta target of $20 adjusted EPS in 2027, and the Sandals stake will be included in 2027 guidance once the deal closes in early 2027.
- **Stock reaction:** Shares opened up about 7.6% at $301.81, the day's top mover, and pared the gain to about 5.5% by the close. [Royal Caribbean Q3 2026: Six Straight EPS Beats and a Raised Outlook](https://247wallst.com/cards/royal-caribbean-cruises-q3-2026-earnings-rcl-01m5yf0swjskp7b1gj0p2q6as5)
