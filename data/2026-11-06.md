# Cruise brief — Fri Nov 6, 2026
*Covering Thu Nov 5, 2026*

## Top 3
1. Royal Caribbean opened its summer 2028 Caribbean program, which runs April–October with 11 ships. Four are Icon-class: Hero from Miami, Legend from Fort Lauderdale, Star from Port Canaveral and Icon from Galveston. [Royal Caribbean Unveils 2028 Caribbean Lineup with Four Icon-Class Ships](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/royal-caribbean-unveils-2028-caribbean-lineup-with-four-icon-class-ships/)
2. Carnival opened more than 300 Port Canaveral departures for 2028–29 on Breeze, Firenze, Festivale, Glory and Mardi Gras. They include more than 200 calls at Celebration Key, and every Glory sailing stops there. [Carnival Unveils 2028-29 Port Canaveral Lineup](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/carnival-unveils-2028-29-port-canaveral-lineup/)
3. Norwegian Cruise Line opened its Black Friday sale with 50% off every cruise and the return of the Free at Sea package. The line values Free at Sea at more than $2,000; it includes an open bar, specialty dining, shore excursion credits and Wi-Fi. [Norwegian Cruise Line Gives "Cruise-Curious" Travelers 10 Reasons to Sail](https://www.nclhltd.com/news-media/press-releases/detail/838/norwegian-cruise-line-gives-cruise-curious-travelers-10)
