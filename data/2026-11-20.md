# Cruise brief — Fri Nov 20, 2026
*Covering Thu Nov 19 – Fri Nov 20, 2026*

## Top 3
1. [CCL] Carnival released new details on its next-generation Ace Class, first named in July with Carnival Destiny. The ships are smaller than recent Carnival builds: LNG-powered, about 130,000 gross tons with roughly 1,650 cabins, and sized to reach more ports. Fincantieri will build two at Marghera for delivery in 2033 and 2035 under a contract worth more than €2 billion, and the design is meant to work for several Carnival Corp brands. [Next Gen Carnival Ships Downsize to 3,300 Guests](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/next-gen-carnival-ships-downsize-to-3300-guests/)
2. Carnival is also expanding Celebration Key by 6,000 guests of land-side capacity, in two phases in 2028 and 2030. The 2030 phase adds a water park with 18 waterslides, a lazy river and an infinity pool. Carnival Destiny, due in 2029, will have eight pools, more than any other ship in the fleet. [Carnival Cruise Line Announces New Ship Class, Celebration Key Expansion](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/carnival-cruise-line-announces-new-ship-class-celebration-key-expansion/)
3. Holland America will send Eurodam to Asia for the first time in 2028–29. The season runs September 2028 to May 2029, with 11- to 27-day sailings from Tokyo, Hong Kong and Singapore, five new ports, and up to $400 in onboard credit for Mariner Society members who book early. [Eurodam to Make Asia Debut for Holland America in 2028-29](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/eurodam-to-make-asia-debut-for-holland-america-in-2028-29/)

## Royal Caribbean Group
High winds meant Oasis of the Seas and Utopia of the Seas missed both Nassau and Perfect Day CocoCay on their Nov 16 four-night sailings, so each spent the whole cruise at sea. Oasis guests got $50 to $150 in onboard credit per cabin, and Utopia came back to Port Canaveral early. [High Winds Leave Two Royal Caribbean Ships on Cruises to Nowhere](https://www.cruisehive.com/high-winds-leave-two-royal-caribbean-ships-on-cruises-to-nowhere/224052)
