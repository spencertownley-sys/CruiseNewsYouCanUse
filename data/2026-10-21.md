# Cruise brief — Wed Oct 21, 2026

## Top 3
1. [NCLH] NCL named its sixth and final Prima-class ship Norwegian Bella and opened bookings. It debuts in May 2028 with seven-night Mediterranean round trips from Barcelona, has 159 Haven suites, and has pool decks more than 15% larger than Aqua's and Luna's. [Norwegian Bella to Debut in Europe with New Onboard Features](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/norwegian-bella-to-debut-in-europe-with-new-onboard-features/)
2. Seabourn launched "Earth & Ocean," a fleet-wide food philosophy built around regional market ingredients. It adds scenic-cruising brunches, seafood boils, fresh pasta and a bigger caviar program, with chef-led shore experiences to follow. [Seabourn Introduces Earth & Ocean Culinary Philosophy](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/seabourn-introduces-earth-ocean-culinary-philosophy/)
3. Carnival Spirit's repositioning voyage from Seattle to Mobile is down to a single port call. Puntarenas was dropped after the detour around Hurricane Trudy, and Cabo had already been lost to a one-day delay leaving Seattle. [Carnival Spirit Down to Single Port Call on 15-Night Repositioning Cruise](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/carnival-spirit-down-to-single-port-call-on-15-night-repositioning-cruise/)
