## Top 3
1. Royal Caribbean formally named Legend of the Seas at Port Everglades on Veterans Day, with retired Air Force Gen. Lori J. Robinson as godmother, and sent it out on a three-night naming cruise. [Royal Caribbean Celebrates Legend of the Seas in Veterans Day Naming Ceremony](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbean-celebrates-legend-of-the-seas-in-veterans-day-naming-ceremony-302937979.html)
2. Crews sank the SS United States about 20 nautical miles off Destin-Fort Walton Beach on Nov 11, making the 1952 liner the world's largest artificial reef. Without explosives, the ship took about five hours to go down. [SS United States sinks off Florida's Gulf Coast](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ss-united-states-sinking-ocean-floor-largest-artificial-reef-world-florida-gulf-coast/)
3. Cunard's Queen Victoria came out of a three-week refit in Rotterdam with a refreshed Grand Lobby, Queens Room and Grill Suites, a new wellness café and eight more Britannia cabins. Its 109-night world cruise departs Jan 10, 2027. [Refreshed Queen Victoria Emerges from Rotterdam Drydock](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/refreshed-queen-victoria-emerges-from-rotterdam-drydock/)
