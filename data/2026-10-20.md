## Top 3
1. Seattle's 2026 cruise season, which ended Oct 18, set records: 330 calls, nearly 2 million guests and about $1.2B in regional economic benefit. A record share of ships used shore power, and Star Princess carried out the port's first LNG bunkering. [Port of Seattle Celebrates Record Cruise Season](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/port-of-seattle-celebrates-record-cruise-season/)
2. [RCL] Royal Caribbean is making China the hub of a wider Asia strategy. Spectrum of the Seas will run more than 70 sailings from Shanghai and Hong Kong in 2026–27, and Navigator of the Seas goes year-round in Singapore. [Royal Caribbean Places China at Center of Asia Strategy](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/royal-caribbean-places-china-at-center-of-asia-strategy/)

The Dec 18, 2027 maiden voyage had already sold out during the loyalty presale, and 28 of the 29 inaugural sailings from Port Everglades were still available. [Disney Believe Bookings Open to General Public Following Maiden Voyage Sellout](https://blogmickey.com/2026/10/disney-believe-bookings-open-to-general-public-following-maiden-voyage-sellout/)
