## Top 3
1. [CCL] Carnival launched four pre-cruise FunPacks (Kidstarter, Zero Proof, Social, Epic), priced from $25 to $99 per person per day and claimed to save 35–55%. Separately, drink packages and WiFi move to dynamic pricing from Dec 1. [Carnival Launches FunPacks Combo Packages](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/carnival-launches-funpacks-combo-packages-for-perks-and-experiences/)
2. [RCL] Royal Caribbean will run its first year-round Europe program in 2028–29, using seven ships with Rhapsody of the Seas wintering in the Canaries, Madeira and Morocco. Crown & Anchor members got early access, with general sale opening Oct 15. [Royal Caribbean Going Year-Round in Europe in 2028-29](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/royal-caribbean-going-year-round-in-europe-in-2028-29/)
3. Disney revealed the name of Disney Cruise Line Japan's first ship: Disney Imagination, a Wish-class vessel building at Meyer Werft that homeports in Tokyo from spring 2029. [Disney Imagination Revealed as Name](https://www.cruisehive.com/disney-imagination-revealed-as-name-of-disney-cruise-line-japans-first-ship/220741)
