# Cruise brief — Wed Oct 14, 2026
*Covering Tue Oct 13, 2026*

## Top 3
1. [CCL] Princess opened its 2028–29 Americas program: 250+ departures, with the Caribbean the largest share at 141. It also returns to the Amazon for the first time since 2014 on a 64-day Circle South America voyage aboard Coral Princess. [Princess Cruises press release](https://www.princess.com/en-au/news/news-releases/2026/10/princess-cruises-unveils-expansive-2028-2029-americas-season-with-return-to-the-amazon)
2. [CCL] Carnival Corp. renewed its favoured-customer repair and refit agreement with Singapore yard Seatrium, extending a relationship that dates to 2010. Royal Princess, Carnival Luminosa and Seabourn Encore are already booked into the yard for Q4. [Carnival Corp. and Seatrium extend 15-year partnership](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/shipbuilding-equipment/carnival-corporation-and-seatrium-extend-15-year-partnership)
3. [RCL] The Hellenic Coast Guard released video of last week's rescue of the woman who went overboard from Odyssey of the Seas. It shows her alive in a life ring after roughly 20 hours in the Ionian Sea. [Video Shows Woman Overboard from Odyssey of the Seas Found Alive in the Dark](https://www.cruisehive.com/video-shows-odyssey-of-the-seas-overboard-woman-found-alive-in-the-dark/220595)
