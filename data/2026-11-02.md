# Cruise brief — Mon Nov 2, 2026
*Covering Fri Oct 30 – Sun Nov 1, 2026*

## Top 3
1. Royal Caribbean has cancelled Labadee calls through at least April 2028, extending a pause that previously ran to mid-2027. Nine ships are affected, and their calls are being swapped for Nassau, San Juan, Cozumel and other ports, or for sea days. [Royal Caribbean Extends Labadee Closure Through April 2028](https://www.cruisehive.com/royal-caribbean-extends-labadee-closure-through-april-2028/222253)
2. Emerald Princess scored 78 on an Oct 16 CDC sanitation inspection in Long Beach, its first failing grade since it entered service in 2007; it scored 99 in May. The report lists 39 violations, including improper food cooling and flies in food areas. [Emerald Princess Fails CDC Inspection for the First Time in 19 Years](https://www.cruisehive.com/emerald-princess-fails-cdc-inspection-for-the-first-time-in-19-years/222329)
3. [RCL] Analysts see upside to Royal Caribbean's 2027 target of about $20 adjusted EPS. Jefferies kept its Buy rating, estimates $20.90 and raised its price target to $350 from $330; William Blair lifted its 2027 estimate to $20.48; and BNP Paribas held its target at $392. [Analysts See Upside in Royal Caribbean's $20 EPS Target for 2027](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/analysts-see-upside-in-royal-caribbeans-20-eps-target-for-2027/)

## Carnival Corp & brands

### Carnival Cruise Line
Carnival is reminding guests of its scooter rules before they sail. Scooters must fit inside the stateroom, and a scooter that does not fit at check-in can mean the guest is denied boarding with no compensation. [Carnival Warns Scooter Users They Could Be Denied Boarding](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-warns-scooter-users-they-could-be-denied-boarding/222231)

### Princess
Royal Princess finished a two-week drydock in Singapore (Oct 16–30) and came out with the Sanctuary Collection. The work added 20 cabins, including six mini-suites, a Sanctuary Restaurant, and an O'Malley's Irish Pub in place of Vines. The ship is now heading to Sydney. [Royal Princess Emerges From Singapore Dry Dock With New Sanctuary Collection](https://www.cruisehive.com/royal-princess-emerges-from-singapore-dry-dock-with-new-sanctuary-collection/222272)
