# Cruise brief — Fri Nov 27, 2026
*Covering Thu Nov 26 – Fri Nov 27, 2026*

## Top 3
1. Royal Caribbean held a ceremony on Nov 24 to welcome Navigator of the Seas to Singapore, where it will be based through October 2027. It sails 3- to 5-night trips to Penang, Langkawi and Phuket, plus longer sailings to Ho Chi Minh City and Bangkok. [Royal Caribbean Marks Navigator of the Seas' Singapore Arrival](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/royal-caribbean-marks-navigator-of-the-seas-singapore-arrival-with-ceremony/)
2. AIDAcosma finished a two-week drydock in Marseille, with Azipod and stabilizer work, a biocide-free hull coating and a new Best Pizza@Sea restaurant, and now heads to the Canaries for winter. AIDAmar enters drydock Dec 20 and AIDAblu in spring 2027. [AIDAcosma Set for Winter Season After Drydock Stay](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/aidacosma-set-for-winter-season-after-drydock-stay/)
3. Royal Caribbean's 2026–27 Australia season is underway with Quantum of the Seas sailing from Brisbane and Anthem of the Seas from Sydney. Next season grows to three ships, with Ovation and Voyager of the Seas joining Anthem. [Royal Caribbean's 2026-27 Season in Australia in Full Swing](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/royal-caribbeans-2026-27-season-in-australia-in-full-swing/)
