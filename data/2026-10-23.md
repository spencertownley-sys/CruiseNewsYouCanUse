# Cruise brief — Fri Oct 23, 2026
*Covering Thu Oct 22, 2026*

## Top 3
1. [CCL] Princess is expanding its Sanctuary Collection premium tier, which includes the Princess Premier package and a private club, pool and restaurant, to five Royal-class ships. Royal Princess went on sale Oct 22. Regal follows Nov 5, then Discovery (Dec 4), Sky (Dec 10) and Enchanted (Dec 17), with first sailings from January to June 2027. [Princess Cruises Expands Sanctuary Collection](https://www.princess.com/news/news-releases/2026/10/princess-cruises-expands-sanctuary-collection)
2. [NCLH] NCL confirmed that Norwegian Luna is running at reduced speed after a technical issue on its Oct 17 Miami sailing. Its Oct 23 Great Stirrup Cay call became a sea day. Guests posted a photo of the starboard Azipod at reduced output, and NCL says safety is not affected. [NCL Confirms Norwegian Luna Speed Issue, Port Cancelled](https://www.cruisehive.com/ncl-confirms-norwegian-luna-speed-issue-port-cancelled/221635)
3. Storm Austen brought P&O's Arvia, MSC Preziosa and MSC Virtuosa into Southampton early ahead of a Met Office amber wind warning for Friday. P&O's Iona also dropped Bilbao-Getxo, the second port it has lost this sailing after French unrest cost it Le Havre. [Storm Austen Forces Three Cruise Ships Into Southampton Early](https://www.cruisehive.com/storm-austen-forces-three-cruise-ships-into-southampton-early/221580)
