# Cruise brief — Wed Nov 4, 2026
*Covering Tue Nov 3, 2026*

## Top 3
1. MSC cancelled MSC Poesia's 11-night Caribbean sailing after the US Coast Guard detained the ship at PortMiami on Nov 1 for an "expanded examination." MSC cited a regulatory matter it could not resolve in time. Guests get full refunds and can stay aboard until Nov 5, and neither MSC nor the Coast Guard has said what inspectors found. [U.S. Coast Guard Detains MSC Cruise Ship in Miami](https://gcaptain.com/u-s-coast-guard-detains-msc-cruise-ship-in-miami/)
2. [LIND] Lindblad posted the best quarterly result in its history. Q3 revenue rose 12% to $269.2 million, adjusted EBITDA rose 12% to $64.1 million, occupancy reached 91% and net yield per guest night rose 6% to $1,398. [Lindblad Expeditions Reports 2026 Q3 Results](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/lindblad-expeditions-reports-2026-q3-results/)
3. Celebrity opened bookings for Celebrity Xcite, the sixth Edge-series ship, which debuts in 2028 with 7-night Caribbean sailings from Miami. Summer 2028 sailings open Dec 9. [Bookings Open for Celebrity Xcite's 2028 Debut, Joining 9 Caribbean Ships](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/bookings-open-for-celebrity-xcites-2028-debut-joining-9-caribbean-ships/)
