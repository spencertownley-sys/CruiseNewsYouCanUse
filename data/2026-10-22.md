## Top 3
1. [CCL] Cunard unveiled 93 voyages for October 2028 to May 2029, covering 104 destinations in 49 countries. The headline is Queen Mary 2's 500th transatlantic crossing, a 21-night New York round trip with calls at Hamburg, Southampton and Liverpool. World Club early booking opens Nov 3. [Queen Mary 2 to Make History in Liverpool on Landmark 500th Transatlantic Crossing](https://www.carnivalcorp.com/media-center/news-releases/queen-mary-2-to-make-history-in-liverpool-on-landmark-500th-transatlantic-crossing-as-cunard-unveils-93-new-voyages/)
2. [RCL] Royal Caribbean said its gratuity rates have not changed. Its FAQ had briefly shown 20% on drinks and dining instead of 18%, and a fake "no adjustments" letter was circulating at the same time. The corrected FAQ now says those auto-gratuities can be adjusted or removed. [Royal Caribbean Says No Gratuity Change After Slip and Fake Letter](https://www.cruisehive.com/royal-caribbean-says-no-gratuity-change-after-slip-and-fake-letter/221540)
3. Holland America launched "Alaska Heritage Tours" for its 80th Alaska season in 2027. The tours are packaged with 2027 cruises and Cruisetours and include the White Pass & Yukon Route railroad, Riverboat Discovery and the McKinley Explorer. [Holland America Line Celebrates 80 Years in Alaska with Heritage Tours](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/news/2026/October/news-102126-alaska-80-heritage)

New coverage of a Washington state class action, *Martinson v. Princess Cruise Lines*, which has been moved to federal court.
