# Cruise brief — Mon Oct 19, 2026
*Covering Fri Oct 16 – Sun Oct 18, 2026*

## Top 3
1. [RCL] A developing East Coast storm made Royal Caribbean scrap Port Canaveral, Perfect Day CocoCay and Nassau from Oasis of the Seas' Oct 18 sailing from Cape Liberty. The ship now goes to Halifax overnight, and guests get $200–$600 refundable onboard credit per person plus a 50% future cruise credit. [Royal Caribbean Sends Oasis of the Seas to Canada as East Coast Storm Builds](https://www.cruisehive.com/royal-caribbean-sends-oasis-of-the-seas-to-canada-as-east-coast-storm-builds/221248)
2. Hurricane Trudy disrupted Mexican Riviera sailings again. Virgin's Brilliant Lady swapped all three Mexico ports for California ones, its fourth straight weather-hit sailing, and Norwegian Bliss cancelled its Oct 17 Acapulco call. [Brilliant Lady Rerouted Again as Hurricane Trudy Forces Fourth Straight Itinerary Change](https://www.cruisehive.com/brilliant-lady-rerouted-again-as-hurricane-trudy-forces-fourth-straight-itinerary-change/221149)
3. Protests and civil unrest in France made P&O Cruises drop Iona's Oct 18 Le Havre call. The ship moves Vigo up a day and adds an overnight in A Coruña. [P&O Cruises Changes 3 Ports on Iona Cruise as French Unrest Grows](https://www.cruisehive.com/po-cruises-changes-3-ports-on-iona-cruise-as-french-unrest-grows/221221)
