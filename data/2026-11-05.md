# Cruise brief — Thu Nov 5, 2026
*Covering Wed Nov 4, 2026*

## Top 3
1. Speaking at CruiseWorld, Carnival Corp's Marie McKenzie said Princess Cays is now open to competing cruise lines. She stressed that the destination is "not closed": Princess's published itineraries are replacing it with Half Moon Cay and RelaxAway starting this month. [Carnival opens private Bahamas destination to competitors](https://www.travelweekly.com/CruiseWorld-Coverage/Carnival-opens-private-Bahamas-destination-to-competitors)
2. MSC confirmed that a "regulatory matter" cancelled MSC Poesia's 11-night Nov 1 sailing from Miami after the Coast Guard detained the ship. Guests get a full refund plus a future cruise credit worth 100% of their fare. [Regulatory Matter Cancels MSC Poesia Cruise](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/regulatory-matter-cancels-msc-poesia-cruise/)
3. [LIND] On its Q3 call, Lindblad raised full-year net yield growth guidance to 6–7% (from 5.5–6.5%) and said its booking window has stretched from 9.5 to 11.9 months. [Lindblad Per Diems Hit Q3 Record of $1,398 as Occupancy Tops 90 Percent](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/lindblad-per-diems-hit-q3-record-of-1398-as-occupancy-tops-90-percent/)
