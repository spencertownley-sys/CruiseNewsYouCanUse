## Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings
- **The quarter:** Net yield fell 6.5% as reported (6.7% in constant currency), about 220 bps better than guidance, and adjusted EBITDA of $937 million beat the guide by $63 million. CEO John Chidsey credited unusually strong close-in demand. [Late Bookers Lift Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Past Q3 Guidance](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/late-bookers-lift-norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-past-q3-guidance/)
- **Outlook:** Q4 constant-currency net yield guidance is unchanged at about -6.5%, and it assumes late bookings will not be as strong again. The delay of Seven Seas Prestige also weighs on Q4. NCLH expects 2027 net yield growth of 1–2%, weighted to the second half, and its booked position for the first half of 2027 is behind last year, though at higher prices. [Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results/)
- **Costs and balance sheet:** NCLH found another $100 million in run-rate savings, mainly from guest and crew air, bringing the total identified so far to more than $600 million. Net leverage stands at 5.5x. [NCLH Identifies Another $100 Million in Savings, More Coming](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/nclh-identifies-another-100-million-in-savings-more-coming/)
- **Regent:** Delivery of Seven Seas Prestige has slipped to late December, and its first bookable sailing is now Dec 28 from Miami. [Regent Quietly Delays Seven Seas Prestige, Cancels Maiden Voyage](https://www.cruisehive.com/regent-quietly-delays-seven-seas-prestige-cancels-maiden-voyage/222181)
