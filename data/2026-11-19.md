[PortMiami Closes FY 2026 With Record 10.5M Cruise Guests](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/portmiami-closes-fy-2026-with-record-10-5m-cruise-guests/)

[Eva Longoria Named Godmother of Seven Seas Prestige](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/eva-longoria-named-godmother-of-seven-seas-prestige/)
