# Cruise brief — Wed Oct 7, 2026
*Covering roughly Tue Oct 6 – Wed Oct 7, 2026*

## Top 3
1. Carnival Corp is reshuffling ships between its brands. Cunard's Queen Anne moves to P&O Cruises in 2029, and P&O's Arcadia moves to Holland America, which takes HAL to 12 ships. [Carnival Moves Arcadia to Holland America, Queen Anne to P&O](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-moves-arcadia-to-holland-america-queen-anne-to-po/219862)
2. Cruise stocks gave back part of Tuesday's rally. [CCL] fell 1.6% to $26.15, [RCL] fell 2.2% to $282.32 and [NCLH] fell 3.0% to $15.05, and no company-specific catalyst turned up. [Carnival price history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ccl/history/)
3. Disney Believe will homeport at Port Everglades and sail its maiden voyage on Dec 18, 2027. Its headliners are an Encanto stage show and new Moana, Frozen and Zootopia venues, and bookings open Oct 19. [Disney Believe Homeport, Debut Date, and New Venues Revealed](https://www.cruisehive.com/disney-believe-homeport-debut-date-and-new-venues-revealed/219971)

## Carnival Corp & brands

### Carnival Cruise Line
[CCL] slipped 1.6% to $26.15 on above-average volume. [CCL price history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ccl/history/). For bookings made on or after Oct 1, Carnival's revised minor policy requires guests aged 15–20 to stay within three cabins of a guardian, and groups now need one chaperone per five minors instead of one per eight. [Carnival No Longer Lets Guests Ages 18 to 20 Book Cabins Anywhere](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-no-longer-lets-guests-ages-18-to-20-book-cabins-anywhere/219834). John Heald also confirmed that about 15 ships now have non-smoking casino areas, up from 10 in 2024. [Carnival Confirms Full List of Ships With Smoke-Free Casinos](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-confirms-full-list-of-ships-with-smoke-free-casinos/219844)
