## Top 3

## Carnival Corp & brands

### Corporate [CCL]
Three firms trimmed Carnival price targets this week despite maintaining bullish ratings: Mizuho cut to $38 (Outperform), BNP Paribas Exane cut to $31 (Outperform), and Argus cut to $30 (Buy) — all citing Q4 EPS guidance of $0.20 (below the roughly $0.25 Street consensus) and sharply higher fuel costs, even though Carnival just posted record Q3 revenue and raised full-year guidance. [Mizuho price-target cut](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/analyst-carnival-nyse-ccl-stock-price-target-cut-by-mizuho-2026-09-30/)

### Carnival Cruise Line
Hurricane Rachel forced Carnival Panorama to swap out Puerto Vallarta, Mazatlan, La Paz and Cabo San Lucas for extra Ensenada calls and sea days, while Carnival Miracle canceled its Cabo stop while repositioning to Galveston — guests on Panorama got $300 onboard credit per stateroom. [Cruise Hive: Hurricane Rachel ship impacts](https://cruisehive.com/every-cruise-ship-impacted-by-hurricane-rachel-so-far/219479)

### Princess
Grand Princess canceled its October 2 Cabo San Lucas call due to Hurricane Rachel, on top of earlier adjustments from Hurricane Polo — worth flagging for guest communications on current West Coast sailings.
