Cruise brief — Thu Nov 26, 2026

Covering Wed Nov 25 – Thu Nov 26, 2026

Top 3

  1. The CDC's updated count shows the norovirus outbreak on Caribbean Princess's Nov 11 sailing reached 268 cases: 233 of 2,936 guests (7.9%) and 35 crew. The ship returned to Fort Lauderdale on Nov 23. It is Princess's fourth and largest norovirus outbreak of 2026, up from 148 cases reported a week earlier. Caribbean Princess Outbreak Climbs to 268 Cases After CDC Update
  2. VIK Viking took delivery of the 998-guest Viking Libra at Fincantieri's Ancona yard. The ship has hybrid propulsion designed for liquefied hydrogen and fuel cells, and sister ship Viking Astrea is due in 2027. Libra's first season is in the Mediterranean and Northern Europe. Viking Takes Delivery of the Hydrogen-Ready Libra
  3. Dredging of St. Thomas's Charlotte Amalie harbor channel, turning basin and WICO berth starts by Nov 27. Work runs seven days a week in two phases through October 2027, so calls there could be affected. Charlotte Amalie Harbor Dredging to Start This Week
