Cruise brief — Wed Nov 25, 2026

Covering Tue Nov 24 – Wed Nov 25, 2026

Top 3

  1. Chantiers de l'Atlantique delivered MSC World Asia, the third ship in MSC's 215,000-ton, LNG-powered World class, at St. Nazaire on Nov 25. It will be christened at Le Havre and starts weeklong Western Mediterranean sailings on Dec 4, then makes its first Atlantic crossing in November 2027 for a Caribbean season from Martinique, Guadeloupe and Barbados. Chantiers de l'Atlantique Delivers MSC World Asia
  2. Allure of the Seas was released from its overboard search around midday Tuesday and replaced its Nov 25 St. Maarten call with a sea day. The St. Thomas and Puerto Plata calls are unchanged, and the ship returns to Miami on Nov 29. The guest's identity has not been released. Allure of the Seas Released From Overboard Search, St. Maarten Cancelled
  3. RCL CCL Cruise stocks rose for a second day Tuesday. RCL gained about 3.5% to near $318, CCL about 1.8% to around $28.40, and NCLH edged up to about $17.10. Morningstar CCL
