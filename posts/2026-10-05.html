Monday, October 5, 2026

Cruise brief — Mon Oct 5, 2026

Covering roughly Fri Oct 2 – Sun Oct 4, 2026

Top 3

  1. RCL Royal Caribbean shares rallied 17% over the week on growing enthusiasm for the Sandals resorts joint venture; Citi dropped its 90-day upside catalyst watch (Buy, $362 target), saying the move is now priced in.
  2. NCLH Norwegian jumped roughly 3.5% Friday as its $750M senior notes offering (refinancing 2028 debt) closed and sector demand sentiment stayed firm.
  3. Hurricane Rachel kept disrupting Mexican Riviera sailings over the weekend, hitting Carnival and Princess directly — Grand Princess lost its Cabo San Lucas call Oct 2, and Carnival Panorama made a second itinerary change before returning to Long Beach Oct 4.

Carnival Corp & brands

Carnival Cruise Line

Carnival Radiance returned to Long Beach on Oct 3 after a 15-day drydock at Seaspan in Victoria — refreshed interiors, an upgraded Effy jewelry boutique, and a new "From Sea to Shining Sea" bow crest marking the ship's 25th year in service. Carnival Radiance back in Long Beach after drydock (Cruise Industry News)

Carnival Panorama had to drop additional Mexican ports as Hurricane Rachel approached, calling twice at Ensenada instead before returning to Long Beach on Oct 4; affected guests received $300 onboard credit per stateroom. Carnival Panorama: second itinerary change due to hurricane (Cruise Industry News)

Princess

Grand Princess's Cabo San Lucas call — already shifted once to Oct 2 — was cancelled outright as Hurricane Rachel closed in on the Baja coast. Every cruise ship impacted by Hurricane Rachel so far (Cruise Hive)

Seabourn

Seabourn unveiled its 2026–27 Antarctica expedition team: Seabourn Venture and Seabourn Pursuit will run 19 departures from Ushuaia between November and March, each with a 21-member expedition staff of marine biologists, geologists, and polar specialists. Seabourn unveils 2026-27 Antarctica expedition team (Cruise Industry News)

Other Carnival brands (Cunard, AIDA, Costa, P&O)

Nothing new.

Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings

NCLH Shares rose roughly 3.5% Friday as the $750M senior notes offering (due 2031) closed, funding redemption of the 6.125% 2028 notes and paying down credit lines — investors read it as proactive balance-sheet management rather than a dilution worry, with the company reaffirming full-year EPS guidance of about $1.50. NCLH rallies as debt move and cruise demand lift outlook (StocksToTrade)

Royal Caribbean Group

RCL The stock's 17% weekly surge, tied to enthusiasm for the Sandals resorts joint venture, prompted Citi to remove its upside catalyst watch while keeping a Buy rating and $362 price target. Citi removes 90-day catalyst watch on Royal Caribbean after 17% rally (GuruFocus)

Legend of the Seas saw a two-hour embarkation delay in Barcelona on Oct 4 after severe weather warnings (heavy rain, 55–75 mph gusts); no itinerary changes were confirmed. Royal Caribbean news round-up: October 4, 2026 (Royal Caribbean Blog)

Industry-wide

MSC Cruises floated out MSC World Atlantic at its French shipyard — the line's 25th ship, carrying up to 6,758 guests — ahead of a November 2027 debut sailing Caribbean itineraries from Port Canaveral. Port Canaveral's next mega cruise ship (ClickOrlando)

Virgin Voyages' Scarlet Lady broke free from its moorings in Rhodes, Greece on Sept 30 (reported Oct 2) amid heavy swells, clipping a catamaran and two smaller boats; no injuries were reported and the ship returned to berth under its own power with tug assistance.
