Top 3

  1. Royal Caribbean has cancelled Labadee calls through at least April 2028, extending a pause that previously ran to mid-2027. Nine ships are affected, and their calls are being swapped for Nassau, San Juan, Cozumel and other ports, or for sea days. Royal Caribbean Extends Labadee Closure Through April 2028
  2. Emerald Princess scored 78 on an Oct 16 CDC sanitation inspection in Long Beach, its first failing grade since it entered service in 2007; it scored 99 in May. The report lists 39 violations, including improper food cooling and flies in food areas. Emerald Princess Fails CDC Inspection for the First Time in 19 Years
  3. RCL Analysts see upside to Royal Caribbean's 2027 target of about $20 adjusted EPS. Jefferies kept its Buy rating, estimates $20.90 and raised its price target to $350 from $330; William Blair lifted its 2027 estimate to $20.48; and BNP Paribas held its target at $392. Analysts See Upside in Royal Caribbean's $20 EPS Target for 2027

Carnival Corp & brands

Carnival Cruise Line

Carnival is reminding guests of its scooter rules before they sail. Scooters must fit inside the stateroom, and a scooter that does not fit at check-in can mean the guest is denied boarding with no compensation. Carnival Warns Scooter Users They Could Be Denied Boarding

Princess

Royal Princess finished a two-week drydock in Singapore (Oct 16–30) and came out with the Sanctuary Collection. The work added 20 cabins, including six mini-suites, a Sanctuary Restaurant, and an O'Malley's Irish Pub in place of Vines. The ship is now heading to Sydney.
