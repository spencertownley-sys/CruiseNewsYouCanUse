Cruise brief — Tue Nov 24, 2026

Covering Mon Nov 23 – Tue Nov 24, 2026

Top 3

  1. Late Monday, Allure of the Seas turned around in the eastern Bahamas to search for a guest confirmed overboard on its Thanksgiving week Eastern Caribbean cruise. Changes to the St. Maarten, St. Thomas and Puerto Plata calls are expected. Allure of the Seas Turns Around to Search for Overboard Guest
  2. Antigua's prime minister said the government turned down an estimated $75–100 million Royal Caribbean Royal Beach Club at Fort James Beach because the line wanted exclusive access. Antigua is building its own public park on the site instead. Antigua Rejects Royal Caribbean Beach Club Over Exclusive Access Bid
  3. CCL NCLH Cruise stocks bounced back Monday after last week's selloff. NCLH rose about 6% to near $17 and CCL about 5% to near $27.90, alongside a broad market rally. Yahoo Finance CCL
