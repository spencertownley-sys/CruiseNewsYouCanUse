Cruise brief — Mon Oct 12, 2026

Covering roughly Fri Oct 9 afternoon – Sun Oct 11, 2026

Top 3

  1. RCL A 44-year-old passenger went overboard from Royal Caribbean's Odyssey of the Seas between Mykonos and Naples; after a roughly 20-hour, multi-agency search a passing cargo ship spotted her and the Hellenic Coast Guard recovered her conscious, being treated for hypothermia. Woman Found Alive After Royal Caribbean Overboard
  2. RCL A medical emergency on Royal Caribbean's Oasis of the Seas pushed its Cape Liberty return back from 6 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., delaying boarding and the Port Canaveral call on the next sailing; affected guests get reimbursed change fees and rescheduled excursions. Oasis of the Seas Returns Late, Next Cruise Pushed Back
  3. CCL Hurricane Isaias forced schedule and port-call changes on three Carnival ships in the Gulf — Carnival Dream's Galveston turnaround slipped two hours, and Carnival Liberty and Carnival Valor both had shortened or shifted Cozumel calls. Industry-wide

Virgin Voyages swapped all three Mexican ports on Brilliant Lady's October 10 Los Angeles sailing because of tropical storms Rachel and Simon — Cabo San Lucas became Santa Barbara, Mazatlán became San Diego, and Puerto Vallarta became Ensenada — while keeping the October 17 return to LA on schedule. Brilliant Lady Cruise Completely Rerouted by Two Tropical Storms

Passengers aboard Odyssey of the Seas posted real-time updates on Reddit about the overboard search, including the captain's announcement of the rescue roughly 20 hours after the passenger went missing — the thread drew heavy engagement as the story developed overnight.
