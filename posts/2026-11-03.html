Top 3

  1. NCLH NCLH is cutting back on fares that include airfare; CEO John Chidsey called the program "basically a marketing subsidy." Savings on guest air phase in through 2027–28 as existing bookings sail, and crew air savings come mainly in 2027. Along with promo changes, they make up the extra $100 million in run-rate benefits flagged on the Q3 call. NCLH Scales Back Included Airfare in Cost and Yield Push
  2. Barcelona approved a €104.5 million plan for a new Terminal C at Adossat Wharf, with shore power and room for 7,000 passengers, and extended Barcelona Cruise Port's concession to 2050. Terminals A and B will be torn down once it opens, which cuts the port from seven cruise terminals to five. Barcelona Approves New Cruise Terminal in €104.5 Million Port Overhaul
  3. Up to 20.5 inches of rain in parts of Brevard County slowed debarkation at Port Canaveral for Carnival Freedom, Carnival Glory, Norwegian Getaway, Disney Wish and Utopia of the Seas. No departures were reported delayed. Heavy Rain Slows Port Canaveral Cruise Day as Flooding Hits Brevard County
