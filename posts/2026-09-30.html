Corporate (CCL)

Carnival Corporation & plc reported fiscal Q3 2026 results Sep 29: revenue $8.44B (+3.5% YoY, beat), adjusted EPS $1.43 (beat), net income $1.92B, and record 2027 advance bookings alongside record customer deposits of $7.6B.
