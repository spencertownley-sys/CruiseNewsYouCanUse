Cruise brief — Tue Nov 17, 2026

Covering Mon Nov 16 – Tue Nov 17, 2026

Top 3

  1. VIK Viking's Q3 revenue rose 9.2% to $2.18 billion, but adjusted EPS fell to $1.11, about 12% below consensus. Low water on European rivers cost roughly $100 million, cut occupancy to 91.3% and pulled net yield down 0.8%. Bookings for 2027 are running 17% ahead of 2026 at the same point, and the stock was roughly flat on the news. Viking Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
  2. VIK Viking also added four ocean-ship options for 2035–36, bringing its ocean pipeline to 19 ships. Its committed river orderbook rose from 20 to 26 vessels, or 48 including options through 2034. Viking Adds Four Ocean Ship Options and Grows River Orderbook
  3. Sapphire Princess has left Fort Lauderdale for Asia, sailing via the Panama Canal, Los Angeles and Hawaii to Tokyo. It will join Diamond Princess for about 20 Southeast Asia sailings this winter, doubling Princess's capacity in the region. Sapphire Princess Starts Repositioning Voyage to Asia
