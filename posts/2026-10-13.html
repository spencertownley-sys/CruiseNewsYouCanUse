Top 3

RCL Royal Caribbean's Voyager of the Seas dropped Puerto Vallarta from its current Mexican Riviera sailing as Hurricane Simon (weakened to Cat 2, 100 mph) closes in; the ship returns to Cabo San Lucas instead and guests get $50–$250 in onboard credit. Voyager of the Seas Cruise Down to One Port Due to Hurricane Simon
  2. NCLH Norwegian Cruise Line is investigating a phishing campaign using guests' real booking details (name, ship, sail date, booking number) to push fake "verify your card" links; NCL says legitimate emails only come from @ncl.com or @nclcorp.com. Norwegian Cruise Line Investigating Scam Emails That Know Booking Details

The UK's MAIB found ineffective task planning and self-resetting (and therefore inadequate) lift-shaft interlocks led to a crew member's fatal crushing aboard P&O's Arvia last October, and is urging Carnival UK to review the interlock design fleet-wide. MAIB cites planning and lift control failures in fatal accident on P&O cruise ship
