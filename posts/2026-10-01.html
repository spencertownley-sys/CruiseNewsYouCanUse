Cruise brief — Thu Oct 1, 2026

Covering roughly Wed Sep 30 - Thu Oct 1, 2026

Top 3

  1. Analyst price-target cuts kept rolling in on Carnival (Mizuho, BNP Paribas Exane, Argus) even as the Street stays bullish after this week's record Q3 — the disconnect is all about softer Q4 guidance and rising fuel costs.
  2. Hurricane Rachel is forcing itinerary changes across the Pacific Mexico coast, hitting Carnival and Princess ships directly this week.
  3. MSC Cruises became the Global Cruises Partner of the 2027 America's Cup in Naples, the latest big sports-sponsorship play in the industry.

Carnival Corp & brands

Corporate CCL

Three firms trimmed Carnival price targets this week despite maintaining bullish ratings: Mizuho cut to $38 (Outperform), BNP Paribas Exane cut to $31 (Outperform), and Argus cut to $30 (Buy) — all citing Q4 EPS guidance of $0.20 (below the roughly $0.25 Street consensus) and sharply higher fuel costs, even though Carnival just posted record Q3 revenue and raised full-year guidance. Mizuho price-target cut

Carnival Cruise Line

Hurricane Rachel forced Carnival Panorama to swap out Puerto Vallarta, Mazatlan, La Paz and Cabo San Lucas for extra Ensenada calls and sea days, while Carnival Miracle canceled its Cabo stop while repositioning to Galveston — guests on Panorama got $300 onboard credit per stateroom.
