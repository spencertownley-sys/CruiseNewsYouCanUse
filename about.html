About Cruise News You Can Use

What this site is, how it is made, and how to tell us about a mistake.

What this is

Cruise News You Can Use is a daily digest of cruise-industry news: earnings, bookings, itinerary changes, incidents, and cruise-line stock moves. Every day's brief is kept on the site and is searchable from the home page.

How briefs are made

Each brief is compiled automatically by an AI-assisted scheduled routine from public news reports and stock data, then published without line-by-line human editing. Not advice

Cruise News You Can Use is an automated news digest for general information only. It is not financial, investment, legal or travel advice. Share prices, analyst ratings and price targets are reported from the linked sources, may be out of date or wrong, and are not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. Check the original source and speak to a licensed financial adviser before making an investment decision.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake, report it on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed) and say which brief and which item. Confirmed errors are fixed in the next run.
