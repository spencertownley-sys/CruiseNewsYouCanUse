# Higgsfield generations for World 1

Every tile, block, prop and parallax layer in 1-1 is cut from these images by
`scripts/gen-art.sh`. Character, enemy and item sprites come from the concept sheets in
`art/concept` (also Higgsfield). Nothing in the level is hand-drawn.

Model: Nano Banana (Higgsfield), 2K, with the style recipe from `docs/05_ASSET_MANIFEST.md §F`.
Files here are 2048 px JPEG copies of the originals.
